01 • ABOUT SJSS

Our School

Shree Janaki Second School (SJSS) is located at Lalbandi-7, Sarlahi, Nepal. The school was established in 2024 and offers education from PG to Class 11/+2.

Our aim is to provide a safe, disciplined and supportive environment where students can learn, grow and prepare for their future.
